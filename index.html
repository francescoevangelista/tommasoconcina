Bio

Born in New York, Tommaso Concina is a Rome based creative director and stylist, working internationally.

During 25 years based in NYC he styled fashion editorials for Esquire, GQ, Style Corriere della Sera, Surface, D La Repubblica and advertising clients including Rolex, Nokia, L'Oréal, Sony. He simultaneously specialised in digital media, from interactive commerce to publishing, brand consulting and web design.

He is currently Professor in Fashion Design and Art Direction, Graduate and Master, at NABA, Nuova Accademia di Belle Arti.
